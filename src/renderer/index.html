🔧 Quick Troubleshooting

  • Discord not showing status: Check your Client ID and Discord privacy settings
  • Can't connect to Roon: Ensure Roon Core is running and accept the pairing request
  • No album artwork: Verify your Imgur Client ID or check internet connection
  • Spotify links not working: Double-check both Client ID and Client Secret
