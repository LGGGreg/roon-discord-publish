🔍 Overview

Roon Discord Rich Presence bridges your Roon music player with Discord, showing your currently playing music as your Discord status. This allows your friends to see what you're listening to in real-time.

Required Services

  • Discord: Required for displaying your music status
  • Roon: Your music player (Roon Core must be running)

Optional Services

  • Spotify: Enables links to tracks on Spotify
  • Imgur: Hosts album artwork for better Discord integration
💡 Tip: You only need Discord and Roon to get started. Spotify and Imgur enhance the experience but aren't required.

🎮 Discord Setup

To display your music status in Discord, you need to create a Discord application and get a Client ID.

Step 1: Create a Discord Application

  1. Go to https://discord.com/developers/applications
  2. Click "New Application"
  3. Enter a name like My Music Status or Roon Rich Presence
  4. Click "Create"

Step 2: Get Your Application ID

  1. In your new application, go to the "General Information" tab
  2. Copy the "Application ID"
  3. Paste this ID into the Discord Client ID field in the app configuration
✅ That's it! Discord setup is complete. Your music status will now appear in Discord when you're playing music through Roon.

Customizing Your Rich Presence

You can customize how your music appears in Discord:

  1. In the Discord Developer Portal, go to "Rich Presence" → "Art Assets"
  2. Upload custom images for different states (playing, paused, etc.)
  3. Note the asset names and configure them in the app if needed

🎵 Roon Configuration

Roon setup is now fully automatic! The app will automatically discover and connect to your Roon Core.

Automatic Setup (Default)

  1. Make sure your Roon Core is running on the same network
  2. Start this application
  3. The app will automatically discover your Roon Core
  4. In Roon, you'll see a pairing request - click "Accept"
  5. The app will automatically detect the currently playing track from any zone

What You'll See

  • Status Page: Shows connection status and currently playing track
  • Now Playing: Displays track info, artist, album, and artwork
  • Automatic Zone Detection: Monitors all zones and shows the currently playing track
✅ That's it! No manual configuration needed. The app automatically handles zone detection and track monitoring.
⚠️ Important: Your Roon Core must be running and accessible on the network for the app to work.

🎧 Spotify Setup (Optional)

Adding Spotify integration allows the app to include Spotify links in your Discord status, making it easy for friends to listen to the same music.

Step 1: Create a Spotify App

  1. Go to https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard
  2. Log in with your Spotify account
  3. Click "Create an App"
  4. Fill in the app details and create the app

Step 2: Get Your Credentials

  1. In your new app, copy the "Client ID"
  2. Click "Show Client Secret" and copy the "Client Secret"
  3. Go to the Configuration tab in this app
  4. Enter the Client ID in the "Spotify Client ID" field
  5. Enter the Client Secret in the "Spotify Client Secret" field
💡 Note: Spotify integration is optional. Without it, your Discord status will still show what you're playing, just without Spotify links.

🖼️ Imgur Setup (Optional)

Imgur integration uploads album artwork to display beautiful cover art in your Discord status.

Step 1: Register an Imgur Application

  1. Go to https://api.imgur.com/oauth2/addclient
  2. Log in or create an Imgur account
  3. Create an application and get your Client ID

Step 2: Configure in App

  1. Copy your Imgur Client ID
  2. Go to the Configuration tab in this app
  3. Paste it into the "Imgur Client ID" field
⚠️ Privacy Note: Album artwork will be uploaded to Imgur and will be publicly accessible (but not easily discoverable). If you prefer not to upload artwork, leave this field empty.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Discord Not Showing Status

  • Verify your Discord Client ID is correct
  • Make sure Discord is running and you're logged in
  • Check that "Display current activity as status message" is enabled in Discord Settings → Activity Privacy
  • Restart both Discord and this app

Can't Connect to Roon

  • Ensure Roon Core is running and on the same network
  • Try disabling "Use Discovery" and entering the Roon Core IP manually
  • Check your firewall settings - Roon uses port 9100-9200
  • Make sure you accepted the pairing request in Roon

No Album Artwork

  • Verify your Imgur Client ID is correct
  • Check your internet connection
  • Some albums may not have artwork available
  • Try clearing the app cache and restarting

Spotify Links Not Working

  • Verify both Spotify Client ID and Client Secret are correct
  • Make sure the track is available on Spotify
  • Some tracks may not be found due to different metadata
💡 Still having issues? Check the Logs tab in the app for detailed error messages that can help identify the problem.
